# About Me

Hello! I’m **Nancy Chi**, a nutrition and public health researcher with an **M.S. in Nutrition** from the Friedman School of Nutrition Science and Policy at Tufts University.

My work focuses on applying quantitative and mixed-methods research to nutrition, food systems, and community health. I have experience analyzing health and program data using **R, SAS, Excel, Tableau, ArcGIS, and NVivo**, with projects related to Food Is Medicine, farmer training, land access, and dietary assessment.

I am particularly interested in translating complex data into clear visualizations and practical insights that support programs, communities, and policy decisions.
