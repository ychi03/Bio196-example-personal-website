<h4 class="research-title">Multivitamin Use and Aging Using NHANES</h4>
<p class="research-desc"><strong>In development.</strong> This study uses National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey data to examine associations between multivitamin use and epigenetic clock aging outcomes among U.S. adults aged 50 and older. The project includes cleaning and analyzing dietary recall and supplement data in SAS and R, calculating Healthy Eating Index scores, and maintaining reproducible data-processing and quality-control workflows.</p>
